Trade Management

			
HINT: Drag the entry, stop loss or take profit lines

			

				An example of trade management, drawn the way TradingView does: a solid
				entry line with a profit and loss badge, dashed stop loss and take
				profit lines, a filled zone between the entry and each exit level, and
				a price axis label on every level. Each line can be dragged directly
				on the chart. The current profit and loss is measured against the last
				bar and updates as new data arrives.
